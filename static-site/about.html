Blood Donation Eligibility & Medical Guidelines

Before donating, please review the safety and medical criteria enforced by our system. These parameters protect both the generous donor and the recipient patient.

Parameter Requirement Rationale / Medical Rule
Age 18 to 65 years Must be an adult of legal consent with safe physiological donor capacity.
Hemoglobin (Hb) ≥ 12.5 g/dL Prevents post-donation donor fatigue and ensures potent packed red blood cells.
Donation Interval Minimum 90 days Enables complete replenishment of iron reserves and red cell mass before next intake.
Donation Shelf Life 35 Days Maximum Whole blood units expire automatically 35 days post collection to ensure patient safety.
Screening State Mandatory Pass Units undergo infectious screening (HIV, Hepatitis, Syphilis). Only Pass units enter inventory.
